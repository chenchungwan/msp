About My Smile Project

My Smile Project is a non-profit dedicated to creating happiness, one smile at a time.

Finding happiness is a pursuit common to all of us. The lucky ones, natural optimists, find happiness easily. For others, myself included, it is a challenging endeavor.

Countless theories, practices, and products promise to make you happy. I've tried a lot of them. Many of them work. However, the easiest, fastest, and most effective one is to just SMILE!

Like any activity, to be proficient one must practice frequently to develop expertise and “muscle” memory.

Inspired by TED talks given by Ron Gutman and David Steindl-Rast, I started this project to help those of us who have a tendency to see the glass half empty: to remind ourselves to stop, smile, be grateful and be happy.
