Smiles

Swipe through smiles from everywhere.

Every photo in the app was shared by someone who wanted to make a stranger smile. Swipe for the next one, or shuffle for a surprise.

Share

Pass one on.

Pick a photo that made you smile and add a caption if you like. It joins the pool and may brighten someone's day as their next reminder. You can take it back any time from the Mine tab.

Smile Map

See where the smiles come from.

If you choose to, your smile appears as a flower on a world map, placed by your approximate area (rounded to about 1 km). Leave the switch off and it stays off the map.

Settings

Quiet when you need quiet.

Choose 1 to 10 smiles a day and the hours they can arrive. Turn reminders off whenever you like.

Made to be kind, including with your data.

No sign-up

There's no name, email or password. The app gives you an anonymous ID so you can manage your own photos.

Only the photo you pick

The app uses Apple's photo picker, so it never sees the rest of your library.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics, and nothing sold. Read the Privacy Policy.
