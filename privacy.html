Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The short version

1. About this policy

This policy explains what data the Smile Project app and website (“Smile Project”, “the app”) handle, why, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Smile Project. It applies together with our Terms and Conditions.

2. What we don't collect

Smile Project is built so that it doesn't need to know who you are. We don't collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, postal address or Apple ID;
  • your contacts, calendar, messages or the rest of your photo library;
  • your precise location, or your location at any time other than when you put a smile on the map;
  • your advertising identifier, device identifiers for tracking, or your activity in other apps and websites;
  • analytics about which screens you open or how you use the app; or
  • payment information (the app is free).

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking, as Apple defines tracking.

3. What you choose to share

Sharing a photo is optional. When you share one, the following is stored in our database and becomes public to everyone who uses the app:

DataDetails
The photoOnly the photo you pick, resized on your phone to a smaller image and a thumbnail before it's sent.
CaptionOnly if you write one.
Approximate locationOnly if you turn on “Put my smile on the Smile Map” for that photo and allow location access. Your phone rounds the position to two decimal places (about 1 km) before sending it; your precise location never leaves your phone. It's shown publicly as a flower on the map.
Time sharedWhen you shared it, to order smiles and count new ones on the map.

Because everything you share is public, please follow section 14 and our Terms.

4. Your anonymous ID

When you first open the app, it signs you in anonymously and receives a random ID. The ID isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and other users never see it. We use it only to:

  • mark which photos are yours, so only you can remove them; and
  • record who flagged a photo, so flagging can't be abused.

5. Flagging a photo

When anyone flags a photo, it's removed from the app for everyone immediately: from the stream, the Smile Map and future reminders. We keep a private copy of the flagged photo (with its caption, location and owner's anonymous ID), plus the flagging user's anonymous ID, the reason and the time. No user can see it. We use it only to review the flag, handle any rights complaint or legal requirement, and prevent misuse of flagging.

6. App performance data

Other than what you choose to share, the only data we receive is anonymous technical data that helps us keep the app working well:

  • Crash reports and diagnostics from Apple, only if you've turned on Share With App Developers in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple sends these without identifying you. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Service logs from our hosting provider (Google Firebase), such as the time of a request, whether it succeeded, and error details. These help us find and fix problems. They aren't used to identify you, build a profile, or for any other purpose.

7. What stays on your phone

Some data is kept only on your device and never sent to us: your reminder settings (how many smiles a day and the hours they can arrive), the reminders scheduled on your phone, whether you've finished setting up the app and accepted the Terms, and the list of photos you've flagged. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so we don't run a notification server or collect a push token.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, to show your smile reminders. Optional; change it in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
  • Location (while using the app), asked only the first time you put a smile on the map, and used only at that moment. Optional; the app works fully without it.
  • Photos: no permission is needed. The app uses Apple's photo picker, which gives it only the photo you choose and never access to the rest of your library.
  • Background refresh, so the app can top up your reminders while it's closed. It downloads photos from the shared pool and doesn't send anything about you.

9. How we use data, and what we never do

We use the data described above only to:

  • show shared smiles to people in the app, in reminders and on the Smile Map;
  • let you manage and remove the photos you've shared;
  • remove flagged photos, review them, and keep the app safe and kind;
  • keep the app working and improve its performance (section 6); and
  • meet legal obligations.

We never:

  • sell, rent or trade any data, including usage data, photos, locations or anonymous IDs;
  • share data with advertisers, data brokers or marketing companies;
  • use your photos for any commercial purpose, including advertising, marketing, merchandise, or training artificial intelligence;
  • track you across other apps or websites, or build a profile of you; or
  • try to identify you from your photos, for example with face recognition.

10. Who can see or receive data

  • Everyone using the app can see photos you share, their captions, and, if you chose it, their approximate location on the Smile Map. They may see your photo in a reminder notification on their phone. They can't see your anonymous ID.
  • Google Firebase hosts our database and anonymous sign-in. Google processes data only on our behalf, under the Firebase data processing terms.
  • Apple provides the map, notifications, photo picker and app distribution, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple also provides the optional crash reports in section 6.
  • Authorities or others, only if the law requires it, or if needed to protect someone's safety or to respond to a valid rights complaint about a photo.
  • A new owner, if Smile Project is ever transferred, who must keep the promises in this policy.

11. How long we keep it

  • Photos you share stay until you remove them from the Mine tab, they're flagged, or the app closes. Removing a photo deletes it, its thumbnail, caption and map location from our database.
  • Flagged photos are kept privately only as long as needed for review and any follow-up, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Your anonymous ID stays with the photos you've shared. If you delete the app, the ID is lost from your phone and the app can no longer tell which photos are yours; please remove your photos first, or email us to remove them.
  • Service logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider, typically 30 days.
  • Copies in notifications already delivered to other people's phones are outside our control and remain until those people clear them.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Remove any photo you've shared from the Mine tab at any time.
  • Leave “Put my smile on the Smile Map” off, or turn off location access for the app.
  • Turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings.
  • Turn off Share With App Developers to stop sending crash reports.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete data about you, or to remove a photo, by email (section 18). Because we don't know who you are, please describe the photos and roughly when you shared them so we can find them.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under local law, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation. We honor those rights and won't treat you differently for using them. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it. Where these laws require a legal basis, we rely on your consent for what you choose to share and for location, on providing the service you've asked for, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and working well. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Children

Smile Project is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Our Terms also don't allow photos of children aged 13 or under. If you see such a photo, or believe a child under 13 has shared one, flag it in the app, which removes it immediately, or email us.

14. Personal information in photos

Smiling faces are what Smile Project is about. But because shared photos are public, please don't include personal information in a photo or its caption, such as full names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, license plates, school names, IDs or documents. We don't scan photos or extract anything from them. If you spot personal information in a photo, flag it so it's removed.

15. Security

Data travels between the app and our database over encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Database rules allow only a photo's owner to delete it, except that anyone can flag it, and nobody can read flagged photos from the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share anything you'd want kept private.

16. Where data is stored

Our database is hosted by Google Firebase, which may store and process data in the United States and other countries. Where data moves across borders, it's protected by Google's standard safeguards, including standard contractual clauses.

17. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, such as anything affecting how your photos are used, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies. The promises in this policy won't be weakened for photos and data shared before a change.
